What Comfort Systems USA says it does

_523507"> ITEM 1. Business Comfort Systems USA, Inc., a Delaware corporation, was established in 1997. We provide mechanical and electrical contracting services. Our mechanical segment principally includes heating, ventilation and air conditioning ("HVAC"), plumbing, piping and controls, as well as off-site construction, monitoring and fire protection. Our electrical segment includes installation and servicing of electrical systems. We build, install, maintain, repair and replace mechanical, electrical and plumbing ("MEP") systems through our 50 operating units with 190 locations in 142 cities throughout the United States. We operate primarily in the commercial, industrial and institutional MEP markets and perform most of our services in manufacturing, healthcare, education, office, technology, retail and government facilities.
