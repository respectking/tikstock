What BNY Mellon says it does

Description of Business The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation, a Delaware corporation (NYSE symbol: BK), is a global financial services platforms company headquartered in New York, New York, with $59.3 trillion in assets under custody and/or administration and $2.2 trillion in assets under management as of Dec. 31, 2025. With its subsidiaries, BNY has been in business since 1784. We divide our businesses into three principal business segments: Securities Services, Market and Wealth Services and Investment and Wealth Management. We also have an Other segment, which includes the corporate treasury activities (including our securities portfolio), tax credit investments and other corporate investments, corporate and bank-owned life insurance, derivatives and other trading activity, and certain business exits.
